About these excerpts

Mick Mouse is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Jupiter Broadcasting, its hosts, or its guests.

Each bundled audio file is a 20-second excerpt from 05:00 to 05:20 of the linked original recording. The audio was trimmed and re-encoded to mono MP3 at 44.1 kHz and 64 kbit/s. The catalogue lists the full original episode duration; the demo player contains the short excerpt. Follow an episode source link below to hear the full recording.

These media notices cover the podcast recordings. The application code, Mick Mouse brand, and original cover artwork are separate works; they are not Jupiter Broadcasting material or official show artwork.
